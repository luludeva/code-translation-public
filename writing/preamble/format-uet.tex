% --- Co chu nen 13pt (muc 5) ---
% Lop `report` chuan chi co 10/11/12pt. Goi `fontsize` cho phep dat co bat ky
% va tu tinh lai \large, \small... theo ti le dung.

% --- Font: Times New Roman (muc 5) ---
% TeX Gyre Termes la ban clone metric-compatible cua Times New Roman,

%   \setmainfont{Times New Roman}

% --- Gian dong 1.3 lines (muc 5) ---

% --- Cach doan 6pt, thut dau dong 1cm (muc 5) ---

% --- So trang dat giua, phia duoi (muc 2) ---

% --- Chu giai: bang dat TREN, hinh dat DUOI (muc 4) ---

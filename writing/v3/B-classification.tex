\chapter{Classification of Retained Divergences}\label{app:classes}

This appendix states the rules by which Section~\ref{sec:m-rq1} assigns a retained divergence to the class benign, non-benign or undecided.

\section{Rules}\label{app:c-rules}

The rules are checked in the order of Table~\ref{tab:app-rules}, and the first rule that matches decides. The codes are those used in the records of the harness.


\section{Definitions}\label{app:c-definitions}

Under rule B2, two integer tokens are equivalent only when they are equal. Two decimal tokens are equivalent when they differ by at most one unit in the last decimal digit of the token with fewer digits, or by at most a relative $10^{\pFloatRelTolExp}$; tokens in exponent form are compared by the relative tolerance alone, and a negative zero is equivalent to zero. Rule N4 applies to libraries only, and rules B1, B2, N3 and U-residual apply to the standard output of a library call once its state agrees. When both sides exceed a limit, rules B1, B2 and N3 are applied to the lines of the common prefix of the two outputs, and rule U-residual is not applied.

\section{Classification of a Translation}\label{app:c-aggregation}

A translation is classified from all of its retained divergences over the seeds of the main run. It is non-benign when at least one retained divergence is non-benign, undecided when none is non-benign and at least one is undecided, and benign when all are benign. The range reported in Chapter~\ref{ch:results} counts the non-benign translations at its lower end and adds the undecided translations at its upper end.
